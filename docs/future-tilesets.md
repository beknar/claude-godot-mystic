# Future tilesets

16×16 top-down pixel art tilesets on itch.io that could sit beside the
antarcticbees Painted Lands packs this project uses, ranked best match first,
with which of our packs each could work with:

- **Forest**: The Painted Lands – Forest Tileset
- **Green Caves**: The Painted Lands – Green Caves Tileset
- **Cozy Cottage**: Interior – Cozy Cottage Tileset
- **Farm**: Farm Tileset – 4 Seasons

Researched 2026-09-28 with a few web searches and one read of each shop page
(no scraping, no downloads). Every pack by antarcticbees is already owned
(RPG Characters, NPC Characters, Cozy Cottage, Farm – 4 Seasons, Green Caves,
Forest), so none are listed here.

## What "matches" means here

The Painted Lands look: hand-shaded, painterly 16 px art with soft
highlights, natural and fairly muted greens (Forest lawn `(83, 131, 79)`,
saturation about 0.37), dark but not heavy outlines, and a moderate level of
detail. Farm is a little more saturated (0.47); Green Caves darker and bluer;
Cozy Cottage pastel and light (see `docs/painted-lands-art-fit.md`).

The ranking below comes from each shop page's own description, tags,
reviews, and what the pack contains. The rendered art was not measured.
**Before buying, download the free version or the preview images and run the
same color comparison used for `docs/painted-lands-art-fit.md`** (median RGB
distance to our sheets, the share within 24, and saturation), then place a
few pieces on Forest lawn and the cave floor. Anything that fails that check
drops down this list whatever its rank.

## Before adding any of them

- Buy (or download the free tier) and read the license file in the download.
  Shop pages summarized above say: Mana Seed, PixiVan, Maygetsu (paid tier),
  Winter Town, and Ventilatore premium allow commercial use and modification
  and forbid resale or redistribution; Darkice8899 and ELV need checking.
- Keep purchased packs out of git: copy them into `assets/pack/<name>/`,
  which is ignored, and add a credit in README.md.
- Run the color comparison against our sheets and add the results to
  `docs/painted-lands-art-fit.md`, the reference for what goes with what.
